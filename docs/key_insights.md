# Key Business Insights

1. **Swiggy One members cost 54% less to convert than non-members** (Rs 28 vs Rs 60 simulated CAC), and have a **26% repeat-purchase rate** vs **14%** for non-members — this is the quantified case for shifting marketing budget from acquisition toward loyalty-program enrollment, matching the direction Swiggy has signaled publicly.

2. Among true paid-acquisition channels (excluding retention-only channels like the Swiggy One emailer, which only reaches existing members), **Referral** acquires a customer for Rs 37 vs **Performance Ads (Search/Display)** at Rs 105 — shifting Rs 50,000 of *new-customer* acquisition budget from the weakest to the strongest of these would buy an estimated **872 additional conversions** at the same total spend.

3. Funnel drop-off: of 38,991 messages sent, only 1,985 converted (5.1% overall) — the steepest drop is Opened -> Clicked, pointing at a creative/offer problem, not a targeting problem.

4. RFM segmentation shows most converting customers sit in Potential/At-Risk tiers, not Champions — exactly the group a win-back + Swiggy One upsell campaign should target first, since insight #1 shows membership is the single biggest lever on both cost and repeat behavior.

5. Data-quality note: 8.6% of raw CRM records were duplicates, 48 records had a membership-flag/segment-label mismatch (a two-systems-of-record bug), and roughly 7-8% of raw interactions referenced customers that didn't validate — cleaning this before analysis changed the CAC numbers materially, which is why the governance layer isn't optional.
